Frequently asked questions

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. We're available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. For an emergency, call 514-484-4400: a plumber answers directly.

Which areas do you serve?

We cover the island of Montreal, Laval and the South Shore (Longueuil, Brossard, etc.). Check the list of areas above or call us to confirm.

Are your plumbers certified?

Yes. Our master plumbers hold a licence from the Régie du bâtiment du Québec and are members of the Corporation des maîtres mécaniciens en tuyauterie du Québec (CMMTQ). We are fully insured.

What types of properties do you serve?

Houses, condos, plexes, rental buildings, stores, restaurants, offices and industrial buildings.
